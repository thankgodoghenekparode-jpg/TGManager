### B. Executive Summary

TGManager is a modern, cloud-based school management system built for schools. In one
platform, your school can:

- Enrol students and keep complete academic, guardian and medical records.
- Track attendance via QR gate scanning, class registers and staff clock-in/out.
- Build timetables that stay in sync across every class and teacher.
- Enter continuous assessment and exam scores, apply WAEC-standard grades, run an
  approval workflow and produce printable report cards.
- Issue fee invoices, record part-payments and balances, and give parents official receipts.
- Give parents a login to see results, attendance and fees — ending result-collection queues.
- Send memos and announcements to staff and parents instantly.
- Monitor the whole school from live dashboards.

**Proposed engagement:** a free one-term pilot, followed by a simple per-student, per-term
subscription only if you choose to continue.

### D. Proposed Solution — What TGManager Does

**1. Admissions & Student Records**
Enrol students, assign classes and arms, and keep guardians, medical and academic records
in one searchable place.

**2. Attendance**
QR-based gate scanning, live class registers and daily attendance tracking, with
location capture for gate and classroom attendance.

**3. Timetable**
Create class and teacher timetables that stay consistent across the school.

**4. Grading & Report Cards**
Enter continuous assessment and exam scores, apply your grading scheme, run a formal
approval/publishing workflow, and produce printable, professional report cards.

**5. Fees, Invoices & Receipts**
Define your fee schedule and levies, generate invoices, record full or part-payments,
track balances and record the number of times each fee was paid — with instant official
receipts for parents.

**6. Parent Portal**
Parents log in to view their children's results, attendance and fee status, and to read
school news — no more queues on result day.

**7. Announcements**
Send memos and school news to staff and parents in seconds.

**8. Analytics & Reporting**
Live dashboards for enrolment, collection rate, attendance and academic performance.

**9. Staff & Roles**
Staff accounts with role-based permissions (principal, bursar, exams officer, teachers,
gate officer), plus staff clock-in/out.

**10. Documents & Sharing**
Every document — report cards, invoices, receipts — can be downloaded as a PDF or sent
directly to a parent or colleague on WhatsApp from within the app.
